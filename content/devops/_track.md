@track devops
@name DevOps Engineering
@brief
- 2026 loops test **troubleshooting and trade-off reasoning** more than tool definitions: CI/CD architecture choices, Kubernetes operational fluency, GitOps, observability strategy and live pipeline debugging.
- Many loops include a **live, screen-shared SSH round** on a broken environment. Narrate every step; silence is the most common way to fail it.
- **Kubernetes is expected** for most mid-level roles. CKA is the most respected certificate, but practical answers count for more.
- **Fresh topics to know:** Ingress NGINX was retired in March 2026 (Gateway API is the recommended path), in-place pod resize went GA in Kubernetes 1.35, OpenTelemetry is the default for new instrumentation, and pull-based GitOps is the norm.
- Check whether the role is really **platform engineering** (internal developer platform, Backstage, Crossplane) and tune your prep to it.
@tips
- **Mitigate first, root-cause second.** Users before curiosity.
- **Narrate:** what you check, why, and what you expect to see.
- **Take a position:** "I would pick X here because..." beats listing every tool you have touched.
- **Tie reliability to users:** SLOs and error budgets, not CPU graphs.
- **Incident stories need:** detection time, the fix, and the process change afterwards.
